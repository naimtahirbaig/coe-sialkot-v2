How this test works
  • 10 multiple choice questions (1 mark each) followed by 8 written items: short answers, a translation, a letter, a summary, and sentence-making. Total marks for the paper: 50.
  • Each MCQ is on screen for 25 seconds; it moves on after 15 seconds even if you have not answered. Written items get double the time of a plain MCQ pace, matched to how long that answer should take.
  • Once you leave a question you cannot return to it. An unanswered MCQ is marked zero.
  • Every question — MCQ and written — is marked automatically the moment you finish: MCQs against the correct option, written items by checking your answer against the model answer's key words (or, for sentence-making, whether you actually used each required word in a real sentence). This is a keyword check, not a human reading for meaning, so your teacher should still look over the written answers.
  • The MCQs, their order, and the order of the options change every time this test is opened.
  • Your full result — marks, correct answers, and marking notes for every item — is shown only after the last question.
Camera check required This is a proctored test. Your camera must stay on for the whole paper, and five photographs of you will be taken at random moments and printed on your result card so your teacher can verify who sat the test. The photos stay on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    How the written marks were worked out: MCQs are graded exactly (right option = 1 mark). Every written item is auto-marked by checking your answer for the key words/phrases pulled from its model answer (for sentence-making, by checking that each required word is actually used in a proper sentence, not just typed on its own). This gives a fast, consistent first-pass score out of 50, but it is a keyword check rather than a reading for meaning — a well-written answer that avoids the exact expected wording can score lower than it deserves, and a list of matching buzzwords can score higher than it deserves. Teachers should treat the written-section marks as a draft and adjust them after reading the actual answers.
