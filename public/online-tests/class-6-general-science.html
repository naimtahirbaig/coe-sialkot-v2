How this test works
  • 52 multiple choice questions. Each carries 1 mark. There is no negative marking.
  • Every question is on screen for 15 seconds. When the time runs out the next question appears on its own.
  • Once you leave a question you cannot return to it. An unanswered question is marked zero.
  • The questions, their order and the order of the options change every time this test is opened.
  • Correct answers are shown only after the last question.
Camera check required This is a proctored test. Your camera must stay on for the whole paper, and five photographs of you will be taken at random moments and printed on your result card so your teacher can verify who sat the test. The photos stay on this device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
If the camera cannot start, the page is probably being opened straight from a folder. Ask your teacher to serve the folder locally, for example with python -m http.server, and then open http://localhost:8000.
